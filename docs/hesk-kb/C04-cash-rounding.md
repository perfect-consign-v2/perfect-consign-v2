# Cash rounding (cash-only)

## As of

2026-10-25

## In one sentence

When the mall rounds cash to a chosen step (cent, nickel, dime, or quarter), rounding applies only if the sale is paid entirely in cash.

## When it happens

Pay with cash only on a mall that has a cash round step other than “No cash rounding.”

## What the books / screens do

- Settings → Money: round cash sales to no rounding, $0.01, $0.05, $0.10, or $0.25.
- Difference posts to cash rounding.
- Shows on the register once cash is the only payment, and on the receipt.
- Card, checks, stored value, or any mix with cash stay on the cent.
- Cash handed over above the rounded due is change — not a second tender. Journal debits only the amount due.

## What is refused

Applying cash-step rounding to mixed or non-cash tenders. A step outside 0 / 1 / 5 / 10 / 25.

## How to check

Cash-only sale that is not already on the step: rounded due and cash-rounding line present. Add a card tender: no round.
