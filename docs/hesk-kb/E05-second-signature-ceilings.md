# Second-signature ceilings (discount / void / payout)

## As of

2026-10-29 — cashiers and managers need a second signature; only a manager or cashier may provide it.

## In one sentence

A cashier or manager needs a second staff signature when a discount, tax-free sale, void, waive, or payout exceeds their stored ceiling; an owner does not.

## When it happens

Aggressive discount, tax-free ticket, void, waive, or payout at the register.

## What the books / screens do

- Cashier or manager discount cannot pass the stored percent ceiling unless a second staff member signs as themselves and their ceiling covers it.
- Tax-free sale cannot pass the stored tax-free ceiling without the same second signature.
- Cashier or manager void, waive, or payout needs that second signature.
- The second signer must be a **manager or cashier** (not books, not vendor).
- Audit row names the signing person.
- Owner does not need the second signature.

## What is refused

Completing the restricted action without a covering second signature when the actor is a cashier or manager over ceiling. A books or vendor password as the second sign.

## How to check

Cashier over ceiling without second sign: refused. With manager sign under their ceiling: allowed; audit shows manager. Books password as second sign: refused.
