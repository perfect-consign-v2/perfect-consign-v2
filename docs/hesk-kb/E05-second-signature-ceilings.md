# Second-signature ceilings (discount / void / payout)

## As of

2026-10-25

## In one sentence

A cashier needs a second staff signature when a discount, tax-free sale, void, waive, or payout exceeds their stored ceiling; an owner does not.

## When it happens

Aggressive discount, tax-free ticket, void, waive, or payout at the register.

## What the books / screens do

- Cashier discount cannot pass the stored percent ceiling unless a second staff member signs as themselves and their ceiling covers it.
- Tax-free sale cannot pass the stored tax-free ceiling without the same second signature.
- Cashier void, waive, or payout needs that second signature.
- Audit row names the signing person.
- Owner does not need the second signature.

## What is refused

Completing the restricted action without a covering second signature when the actor is a cashier over ceiling.

## How to check

Cashier over ceiling without second sign: refused. With manager sign under their ceiling: allowed; audit shows manager.
