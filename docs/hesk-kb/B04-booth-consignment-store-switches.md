# Booth / consignment / store-stock switches

## As of

2026-10-25

## In one sentence

Booth rent, consignment, and store stock are separate mall switches; at least one must stay on, and each mode gates what you can stock and charge.

## When it happens

Changing mall settings, adding items, charging rent, or opening booth / wait-list / map pages.

## What the books / screens do

- Booth rent off: no rent charge, pay-vendors does not take rent, booth/rent/wait list/map pages stay closed.
- Consignment off: days on the floor cannot save; consigned intake is blocked while rules apply.
- Store stock off: store pieces cannot be added while items remain, per refuse rules.
- Item placement: booth piece and consigned piece name a vendor; store piece does not.
- Booth piece needs booth rent on and an open assignment; does not expire.
- Consigned piece needs consignment on; may expire.
- Store piece needs store stock on; no store-take override; sale credits store sales, no vendor payable.

## What is refused

- Turning booth rent off while a booth still owes rent.
- Turning consignment off while a consigned item is still on hand.
- Turning store stock off while a store item is still on hand.
- Leaving all three switches off.

## How to check

With rent off, booth pages closed and no rent charges. With only store stock on, a store sale posts no vendor payable.
