# Booth Active vs Inactive

## As of

2026-11-17

## In one sentence

A booth is Active or Inactive depending on whether it is offered; that is separate from who holds it.

## When it happens

Opening the booth list, assigning a booth, editing the map, or marking a booth inactive.

## What the books / screens do

- The booth list opens on Active. Inactive booths are on the Inactive list.
- Make inactive is on an empty booth and on that booth's row. It hides the booth from the default list, from the assignment choices, and from the map. A booth with a current or scheduled assignment does not show Make inactive.
- Make active is on an inactive booth and on the Inactive list. It offers the booth again.
- Occupancy (Available, Occupied, Ending, Incoming) stays a separate filter on the list you are viewing.
- Open rent on a booth whose assignment has already ended does not by itself refuse Inactive.

## What is refused

Cannot set booth to inactive while it has a current or scheduled assignment. End assignments first.

## How to check

Open an empty booth: Status has Make inactive, and its row on the Active list does too. Mark it Inactive: it leaves the default list, the assignment choices, and the map, and it shows under Inactive with Make active. A booth with an open stay, or a stay that has not started, has no Make inactive control and stays Active. A direct attempt still gets the sentence above.
