# Vendor memo (and payable below zero)

## As of

2026-10-25

## In one sentence

A vendor memo adds to or subtracts from what the mall owes that vendor, with a reason; a debit memo may leave payable below zero until later sales pay it down.

## When it happens

Recording a memo before the next payout; viewing the vendor statement.

## What the books / screens do

- Memo is dated before the next payout and shows on the statement.
- Credit memo increases what the mall owes; debit memo decreases it.
- A debit may leave payable below zero. While below zero, the check is zero. Later sales pay that balance down before a check.
- The memo does not charge or pay booth rent.

## What is refused

- A draw, a check, and any other journal (besides that memo rule) still cannot leave the payable below zero.
- Using a memo to adjust rent receivable.

## How to check

Post a debit memo; statement shows it; open payable may be negative; payout check amount is zero until sales restore payable.
