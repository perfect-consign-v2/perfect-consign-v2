# Tax-exempt vendor rules

## As of

2026-10-25

## In one sentence

A vendor is tax exempt only with a resale or tax-exempt number on file; their items then post with no sales tax.

## When it happens

Editing the vendor’s tax-exempt flag, or selling that vendor’s items.

## What the books / screens do

- Tax exempt on requires a resale / tax-exempt number.
- Tax exempt off allows an empty number.
- A tax-exempt vendor’s items have no tax on the sale.
- Ticket-level tax-free (with reason) is separate: it zeros tax for that ticket only.

## What is refused

Turning tax exempt on without a number on file.

## How to check

Sell an item for a tax-exempt vendor: tax lines are zero. Sales-tax worksheet treats exempt sales per waiver / vendor flag.
