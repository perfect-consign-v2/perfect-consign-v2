# Pay vs credit-only vs hold

## As of

2026-10-25

## In one sentence

A vendor is pay, credit only, or hold — only pay vendors are included in a payout run; credit-only rent is handled at the desk.

## When it happens

Editing vendor payout standing, building a payout run, or collecting rent.

## What the books / screens do

- **Pay** — included in the run (settlement / checks as applicable).
- **Credit only** and **hold** — left out of a run.
- Rent for a credit-only vendor is paid at the desk (not taken through the run the same way).

## What is refused

Expecting credit-only or hold vendors to appear on a normal pay run.

## How to check

Build a run: only pay vendors listed. Credit-only vendor’s rent can still be collected at the register/desk.
