# Gift certificate / gift card / store credit basics

## As of

2026-10-25

## In one sentence

Stored value is sold for a tender and redeemed by number; the open balance cannot go below zero.

## When it happens

Selling a gift certificate/card, issuing store credit, or taking one as tender on a sale.

## What the books / screens do

- Sell gift certificate for cash, card, or check: debit tender, credit the certificate liability.
- Balance left on a number = credits minus redemptions.
- On a sale, redeem by number as a tender; charge cannot exceed remainder.
- Sales-tax worksheet lists issued gift certificates, gift cards, and store credit as non-taxable; redemptions stay inside the sale they paid.

## What is refused

Redeeming more than the remaining balance. Using gift certificate / store credit to hold a layaway ticket.

## How to check

Sell $50 cert; redeem $20: remainder $30. Over-redeem refused. Liability report matches open balances.
