# Rent cycle day and prorated move-in charge

## As of

2026-10-25

## In one sentence

Rent periods follow a cycle day (1–28); move-in periods are prorated by days, truncating toward zero.

## When it happens

Setting the mall cycle day, assigning a vendor to a booth mid-period, charging rent, or collecting rent.

## What the books / screens do

- Cycle day 1 = calendar month. Any other day runs from that day through the day before it next month.
- Move-in period: days from start date through end of that period; amount truncates toward zero. Collect rent names those dates on that charge.
- Full period shows its charge date. Leave period is charged in full.
- Sales can pay that period’s rent only up to what the vendor earned; the rest stays receivable and is collected at the register.

## What is refused

Changing the cycle day after a rent charge has been posted.

## How to check

Assign mid-cycle; charge the move-in period; confirm prorated amount and named date range on the charge.
