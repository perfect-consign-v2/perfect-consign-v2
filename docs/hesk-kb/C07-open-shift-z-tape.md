# Open shift required; Z-tape and over/short

## As of

2026-10-25

## In one sentence

Sales and cash drawer activity must name the open shift; close counts cash and may post over/short; each closed shift has a Z-tape.

## When it happens

Opening a register, ringing sales, dropping cash, closing the shift.

## What the books / screens do

- One open shift per register. Opening cash (or bill/coin count) is fixed once opened.
- Sale, cash rent, payout, stored-value issue, cash drop, over/short, and voids of those must name the open shift. No open register → post refused.
- Expected drawer = opening + cash sales + cash rent + stored value sold for cash − cash reversals − cash payouts − cash drops.
- Cash drop moves drawer cash to safe cash (not a payout).
- Close: counted cash vs expected; match → no difference journal; mismatch → confirm, then one journal between cash and cash over/short.
- Card/check differences stay on the shift. Z-tape records counts, expected lines, drops, and stored-value totals.

## What is refused

- Changing opening count after open.
- Changing or deleting a closed shift.
- Deleting the last register or a register a shift points at.
- Using payable or tax as the close plug.

## How to check

Ring a cash sale without a shift: refused. Close with wrong count: over/short journal + Z-tape.
